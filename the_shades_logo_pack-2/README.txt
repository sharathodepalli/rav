The Shades — Logo Pack (v1)

Folder guide:
- Brand/Logo/Primary: official lockups (transparent PNG)
- Brand/Logo/Icon: icon-only marks (transparent PNG)
- Brand/Logo/Icon/Favicons: favicon/app icon sizes
- Brand/Logo/Exports: ready-to-upload social banners

Usage:
- Light backgrounds: use black lockup/icon
- Dark backgrounds: use white lockup/icon
- Favicon/social profile: icon-only
